How to Use

Describe your image

Write a detailed prompt describing what you want. The more specific you are, the better the result. Include details about subject, setting, lighting, and mood.

Pick model and style

Choose SDXL for fast, affordable images or FLUX for higher quality. Select an art style and aspect ratio to match your vision.

Refine with negatives

Use the negative prompt to exclude things you don't want, like 'blurry, low quality, watermark, text'. This sharpens results significantly.

Generate and download

Click Generate and your image appears in seconds. Download it instantly. Not happy? Adjust your prompt and try again.
