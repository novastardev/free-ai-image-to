Token Formula

Self-hosted models: You pay the exact tokens used. No markup.

External models: our_tokens = provider_usd_cost × 100,000 × 1.30

Example: GPT-4o costs $0.0025 per 1K prompt tokens. For 1,000 tokens: $0.0025 × 100,000 × 1.30 = 325 tokens from your balance.

    "tokens_used": 142,      // actual tokens processed
    "tokens_charged": 142,   // tokens deducted from your balance
